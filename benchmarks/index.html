qevedo-compiler

24 circuits (QFT, QAOA, Quantum Volume, GHZ, Heisenberg, adders, Clifford+T, random Hamiltonians) of 8 to 32 qubits, compiled for rz, sx, x and cx; QCC uses no more two-qubit gates than Qiskit on any of them. Gates relative to Qiskit at optimization level 3 (geometric means) and median time, on a 4-vCPU Intel Xeon virtual machine; lower is better.

On a chip. Compiled for a 19-qubit heavy-hex chip, QCC lays out and routes the circuit itself: with no SWAP when the circuit fits, otherwise with SABRE counting each SWAP for what it costs once merged with its neighbours. QCC is slower than Qiskit on the chip (a median of 90 ms against 48 with CX); without one it is faster on 18 of the 24 circuits, but up to 5× slower on Quantum Volume and 3–14× on the Hamiltonians.

Inside Qiskit. As Qiskit's optimization stage, transpile(circuit, backend, optimization_method="qcc"), on a heavy-hex chip: with CX, 2% fewer two-qubit gates and 15% fewer gates in all, up to 61% on a QFT; with CZ, 10% fewer gates in all.

Multi-core mapping

Mapping circuits onto modular quantum computers, cores joined by quantum links, in qevedo-compiler. Benchmarks of Escofet et al. (QFT, adders, random circuits, Quantum Volume, built with Qiskit); fewer teleportations is better.

ComparisonResult
HQA against FGP-rOEE, 105 configurations1.24× fewer teleportations (mean), 7.8× faster (median)
HQA counting link hops, cores in a ring, grid or line1.6–1.9× fewer than FGP-rOEE
HQA with ports against TeleSABRE, its own device and circuits0.26× the EPR pairs, 0.51× the SWAPs (geometric means)
HQA-latency against HQA, 1 to unlimited EPR pairs per link6–12% shorter circuits on average, never longer

HQA is the Hungarian Qubit Assignment of Escofet et al.; HQA-latency and the routing inside cores are ours. TeleSABRE deadlocks on random circuits and Quantum Volume in all 100 of its attempts; on the 8 circuits it completes, HQA with ports uses fewer EPR pairs and fewer SWAPs on every one. Try the mappers in the multi-core playground.

Method, results and caveats: qcc/docs/MULTICORE.md.
